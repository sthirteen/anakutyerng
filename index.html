Before you begin: this test covers grammar, vocabulary, gap-fill, reading, listening, error correction and choosing the right tone for a situation. Four questions are listening questions — your device will read a short recording aloud, so please use headphones or turn the volume up. You may replay each recording up to 3 times. There is no going back once you answer, so read each question carefully. Set aside about 15 minutes and finish in one sitting.

We send a copy of your result to this address, so please check it is correct.

Your result is also sent to your teacher at Anakut Yerng so they can place you in the right English class. It is not shared with anyone else.
